Basic Cyber Security Practices

No system is completely safe, but users can greatly reduce their risk by following good security practices. Most attacks succeed because of weak passwords, outdated software, or human mistakes.

Use Strong Passwords

Create passwords with at least 12 characters. Use a mix of uppercase letters, lowercase letters, numbers, and symbols. Never reuse the same password for multiple accounts.

Enable Two-Factor Authentication

Two-factor authentication (2FA) adds an extra verification step after entering your password. Even if someone steals your password, they will still need the second code to access your account.

Keep Software Updated

Software updates often fix security weaknesses. Update your operating system, browser, antivirus program, and applications regularly.

Avoid Suspicious Links

Do not click unknown links in emails or messages. Check the sender’s address carefully and avoid downloading attachments from unfamiliar sources.

Protection Checklist

  • Use a reputable antivirus or anti-malware program.
  • Back up important files regularly.
  • Use secure Wi-Fi networks and avoid public Wi-Fi for banking.
  • Lock your devices with a password, PIN, or fingerprint.
  • Review app permissions and remove unused applications.
  • Log out from accounts when using shared computers.
