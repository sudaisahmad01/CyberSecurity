Welcome to Cyber Security

Cyber security is the practice of protecting computers, networks, mobile devices, data, and online services from unauthorized access, attacks, damage, or theft. As people use the internet for banking, education, business, communication, and entertainment, cyber security has become one of the most important fields in technology.

Why Cyber Security Matters

Every day, individuals and organizations store sensitive information online. This includes passwords, financial records, personal identity details, business documents, and customer data. Cyber criminals try to steal or misuse this information, so strong security measures are necessary.

Main Goals of Cyber Security

  • Confidentiality: Keeping private information accessible only to authorized users.
  • Integrity: Ensuring data is not changed or damaged by unauthorized people.
  • Availability: Making sure systems and services are available when needed.
  • Authentication: Verifying the identity of users and devices.
  • Recovery: Restoring data and systems after an attack or failure.

Who Needs Cyber Security?

Cyber security is important for everyone. Students need it to protect their accounts and assignments. Businesses need it to protect customer records and financial systems. Governments need it to protect national infrastructure and citizen data. Even ordinary users need it to avoid scams, identity theft, and malware.
