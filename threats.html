Types of Cyber Attacks

Cyber threats are different methods used by attackers to access systems, steal information, disrupt services, or earn money illegally. Understanding these threats is the first step toward avoiding them.

Malware

Malware is harmful software designed to damage or gain access to a computer system. Common types include viruses, worms, trojans, ransomware, and spyware. Malware can spread through infected downloads, email attachments, or unsafe websites.

Phishing

Phishing is a social engineering attack where criminals send fake emails, messages, or websites that appear trustworthy. The goal is to trick users into entering passwords, bank details, or other personal information.

Ransomware

Ransomware encrypts a victim’s files and demands payment to restore access. It can affect individuals, hospitals, schools, and large companies. Regular backups and updated security software can reduce the damage caused by ransomware.

Password Attacks

Attackers may use guessing, dictionary attacks, or automated programs to discover weak passwords. Using long and unique passwords greatly reduces this risk.

Other Important Threats

Threat Description
Denial of Service (DoS) Overloads a website or network so legitimate users cannot access it.
Man-in-the-Middle An attacker secretly intercepts communication between two parties.
SQL Injection Inserts harmful database commands through insecure web forms.
Social Engineering Manipulates people into revealing confidential information.
Data Breach Unauthorized access to private or confidential data.

Warning Signs

  • Unexpected pop-up messages or security warnings.
  • Slow computer performance without a clear reason.
  • Unknown programs installed on your device.
  • Emails asking urgently for passwords or money.
  • Strange login alerts from unknown locations.
